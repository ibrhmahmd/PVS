MEET THE PIXEL SMITHS

A small studio that makes small games about big things — the ocean and the solar system — one pixel at a time.

OUR STORY

We build honest, educational pixel-art games with an old-school spirit: a crab learning about ocean conservation, a ship learning about the solar system. No micro-transactions, no gacha, no fleecing the parents. Just games that teach while you play.

Everything is handcrafted in Python with Pygame Zero, from the sprites to the sound. We believe small games can carry big ideas.
