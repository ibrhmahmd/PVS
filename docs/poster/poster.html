Real science, hidden in play.
Two 16-bit games for ages 5 to 99. Dodge ocean trash. Fly to Jupiter's icy moon. Every fact in them is real.

01Facts stick when you play them.

The problem

A fact in a lesson stays a sentence

"Europa hides an ocean." "Plastic hurts sea turtles." A child hears it, and the next day it is gone. Facts feel far away from play.

Our answer

Make the fact a game moment

In Crab Quest you dodge the trash. In Space X you fly through Europa's ice. Each fact shows up exactly where it makes sense in the story, and nothing in it is invented.
