02 · The levels

Crab Quest: chapters 1–3

Chapter 1

Kitchen Escape

  • GoalJump up 7 kitchen counters to the way out at the top right.
  • ChallengeTiming each jump; no timer, so take your time.
  • StoryA crab is caught in a kitchen and must escape back to the sea.
Chapter 2

Beach Run

  • GoalSurvive 30 seconds, then run into the ocean wave.
  • DangerSeabirds dive from the sky anywhere on the beach.
  • LearnSeabirds hunt crabs on open sand.
Chapter 3

Sea Floor Dodging

  • GoalDodge for 10 seconds, then follow the sea turtle to safety.
  • DangerPlastic bottles and trash sink from the surface.
  • LearnPlastic in the ocean traps sea animals.

02 · The levels

Crab Quest: chapters 4–6

Chapter 4

Shrimp Help

  • GoalPlay the shrimp: collect all 17 algae on 23 platforms.
  • ChallengeA 60-second timer, and the algae are spread high and low.
  • LearnSea animals help each other: the shrimp feeds the tired crab.
Chapter 5

Predator Chase

  • GoalStay alive for 45 seconds.
  • DangerA red predator fish patrols the sea floor; jump over it.
  • LearnSmall animals survive by watching and dodging predators.
Chapter 6

Home Sweet Home

  • GoalRun and jump to your crab family.
  • EndingReunited in a protected coral sanctuary.
  • LearnProtected reefs give sea life a safe home.

02 · The levels

Space X: levels 1–2

Level 1

Launch pad

  • GoalWalk the Hero and collect helmet, ammo crate, lucky football and fuel can, then board the Ship.
  • ChallengeA 30-second countdown. Run out and it is game over.
  • FactRockets carry their own oxygen: there is no air in space to burn fuel.
Level 2

Earth orbit

  • GoalDodge 10 pieces of space junk. The Ship cannot shoot here.
  • Challenge3 hearts; every hit costs one.
  • FactThousands of old satellites and rocket parts circle Earth at 28,000 km/h.

02 · The levels

Space X: levels 3–4

Level 3

Asteroid belt

  • GoalShoot 30 asteroids. The Ship fires a football every half second; Space adds more.
  • ChallengeA rock that hits the Ship or slips past costs a heart, and rocks come faster as you score.
  • FactThe belt sits between Mars and Jupiter; its rocks are far apart, so ships fly through.
Level 4

Europa

  • GoalBreak 12 ice chunks, then scan Europa for 3 seconds.
  • ChallengeEach chunk takes two hits (the first cracks it); one that slips past costs a heart.
  • FactEuropa’s salty ocean holds about twice the water of all Earth’s oceans. Then: Jupiter!
