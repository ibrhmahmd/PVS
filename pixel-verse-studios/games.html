OUR GAMES

Two educational pixel-art games, built in Python with Pygame Zero for the desktop: one about our ocean, one about our solar system.

CRAB QUEST

An ocean-themed educational adventure for ages 5-99. Six chapters, each with its own gameplay style, follow a crab's journey home — from a kitchen counter to a protected coral sanctuary — while raising awareness about marine life and ocean conservation.

SPACE X

Journey to Jupiter: an educational space adventure for Ages 5-99. The Hero's Mission: fly to Europa, Jupiter's icy moon, and find out if its hidden ocean could hold life. Four Levels take the Hero from the launch pad to Jupiter, with a Fact card after each one.

Space adventure Ages 5-99 Python + Pygame Zero

THE MISSION

Four Levels, from the launch pad to Europa. The three flying Levels give you 3 hearts. Clear a Level and a Fact card teaches you something new before the next one.

Explore the full journey
Level 1

LAUNCH PAD

Walk the Hero left and right, collect the 4-item Checklist (helmet, ammo crate, lucky football, fuel can) and board the Ship before the 30-second Countdown ends. Then blast off!

Fact card

Rockets carry their own oxygen, because there is no air in space to burn their fuel.

Level 2

EARTH ORBIT

Dodge 10 pieces of space junk. The Ship cannot shoot here.

Fact card

Thousands of old satellites and rocket parts circle our planet at 28,000 km/h.

Level 3

ASTEROID BELT

Shoot 30 asteroids. The Ship fires footballs by itself every half second, and SPACE adds extra shots. The more you score, the faster the asteroids come.

Fact card

The asteroid belt sits between Mars and Jupiter. It holds millions of space rocks, but they are so far apart that ships can fly through safely.

Level 4

EUROPA

Break 12 ice chunks. Each one takes two hits: the first hit cracks it. Then the Ship scans Europa for 3 seconds: "Ocean found!"

Fact card

Europa's salty ocean holds about twice as much water as all of Earth's oceans.

FACT CARD

After every Level the Hero shares a space fact. Press SPACE to continue.

GAME OVER

Lose all your hearts, or run out of Countdown, and you learn a random fact about the Sun or one of the 8 planets. Press R to try the same Level again.

YOU REACHED JUPITER!

"Mission complete! The Hero scanned Europa's ocean and reached Jupiter." You see your score and high score, plus a fact about Jupiter's Great Red Spot, a storm that has raged for hundreds of years. Press R to fly again.

CONTROLS

Walk or fly with ←/→ or the on-screen LEFT/RIGHT buttons. Press SPACE to start, continue and shoot (the Ship fires by itself in the Asteroid belt and on Europa). Pause with P or the on-screen pause button, press R to retry or fly again, and F for fullscreen.
