OUR GAMES

Two educational pixel-art games, built in Python with Pygame Zero for the desktop: one about our ocean, one about our solar system.

CRAB QUEST

An ocean-themed educational adventure for ages 5-99. Six chapters, each with its own gameplay style, follow a crab's journey home — from a kitchen counter to a protected coral sanctuary — while raising awareness about marine life and ocean conservation.

Adventure Ages 5-99 Python + Pygame Zero

THE HERO'S JOURNEY

Space X has no written story — the loop is the journey. Five beats of the Story Spine, from the ship waiting on the title screen to the fact you learn every time you lose.

The Title Screen

Gameplay

"SpaceX is an educational game to learn about our solar system." Press SPACE to launch.

One day…

Launch

The pilot flies into the asteroid field with three hearts.

Gameplay

Steer with ←/→, fire with SPACE; every asteroid shot scores a point.

Because of that…

Rising Stakes

The more the pilot scores, the harder space fights back.

Gameplay

The level rises every 30 points; asteroids come faster and more often. Any asteroid that hits the ship or slips past costs a heart.

Until finally…

Game Over

The last heart is gone.

Gameplay

Your high score is kept for the next run.

And ever since then…

A New Fact Every Run

Every crash teaches something: the game shows a fact about the Sun or one of the 8 planets, and the pilot relaunches a little wiser.

Gameplay

Press R to relaunch.

CONTROLS

Steer with ←/→, press SPACE to start or fire, P to pause, R to restart after game over, and F for fullscreen.
