THE HERO'S JOURNEY IN 16 BITS

Every story can be told with the Story Spine: "Once upon a time…" then "One day…", "Because of that…", "Until finally…", and "And ever since then…". Here is how each of our games follows it.

CRAB QUEST

Six chapters follow the crab's family story, from a kitchen counter to a protected coral sanctuary.

Once upon a time…

The Reef

A small crab lives among the fish, clams and sea stars of the reef.

One day…

Kitchen Escape (Chapter 1)

The crab finds itself stranded on a kitchen counter, far from the sea.

Gameplay

Jump across 7 platforms to reach the goal at the top right.

Because of that…

Beach Run (Chapter 2)

It has to cross the beach to reach the water, with seabirds diving from above.

Gameplay

Survive 30 seconds of diving birds, then reach the wave.

"THE WAVE IS COMING! GET TO THE WATER!"

Because of that…

Sea Floor Dodging (Chapter 3)

Back underwater, plastic trash rains down — until a sea turtle shows the way.

Gameplay

Dodge falling trash for 10 seconds, then follow the turtle.

"FOLLOW THE TURTLE TO SAFETY!"

Because of that…

Shrimp Help (Chapter 4)

The crab runs out of strength, and a shrimp friend gathers food for it.

Gameplay

Play as the shrimp: collect all 17 algae in 60 seconds.

"CRAB REGAINED STRENGTH!"

Until finally…

Predator Chase (Chapter 5)

A red predator fish hunts the crab across the reef — and the crab outlasts it.

Gameplay

Survive the patrolling fish for 45 seconds.

"SURVIVE THE PATROLLING FISH!"

And ever since then…

Home Sweet Home (Chapter 6)

The crab lives with its family in a protected coral sanctuary.

Gameplay

Run to your family.

"REUNITED! YOU ARE HOME."

SPACE X

An educational space shooter with no written story — the loop is the journey, from launch to the fact you learn every time you lose.

Once upon a time…

The Title Screen

A small ship waits at the edge of the solar system.

Gameplay

"SpaceX is an educational game to learn about our solar system." Press SPACE to launch.

One day…

Launch

The pilot flies into the asteroid field with three hearts.

Gameplay

Steer with ←/→, fire with SPACE; every asteroid shot scores a point.

Because of that…

Rising Stakes

The more the pilot scores, the harder space fights back.

Gameplay

The level rises every 30 points; asteroids come faster and more often. Any asteroid that hits the ship or slips past costs a heart.

Until finally…

Game Over

The last heart is gone.

Gameplay

Your high score is kept for the next run.

And ever since then…

A New Fact Every Run

Every crash teaches something: the game shows a fact about the Sun or one of the 8 planets, and the pilot relaunches a little wiser.

Gameplay

Press R to relaunch.
