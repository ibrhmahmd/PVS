THE HERO'S JOURNEY IN 16 BITS

Every story can be told with the Story Spine: "Once upon a time…" then "One day…", "Because of that…", "Until finally…", and "And ever since then…". Here is how each of our games follows it.

CRAB QUEST

Six chapters follow the crab's family story, from a kitchen counter to a protected coral sanctuary.

Once upon a time…

The Reef

A small crab lives among the fish, clams and sea stars of the reef.

One day…

Kitchen Escape (Chapter 1)

The crab finds itself stranded on a kitchen counter, far from the sea.

Gameplay

Jump across 7 platforms to reach the goal at the top right.

Because of that…

Beach Run (Chapter 2)

It has to cross the beach to reach the water, with seabirds diving from above.

Gameplay

Survive 30 seconds of diving birds, then reach the wave.

"THE WAVE IS COMING! GET TO THE WATER!"

Because of that…

Sea Floor Dodging (Chapter 3)

Back underwater, plastic trash rains down — until a sea turtle shows the way.

Gameplay

Dodge falling trash for 10 seconds, then follow the turtle.

"FOLLOW THE TURTLE TO SAFETY!"

Because of that…

Shrimp Help (Chapter 4)

The crab runs out of strength, and a shrimp friend gathers food for it.

Gameplay

Play as the shrimp: collect all 17 algae in 60 seconds.

"CRAB REGAINED STRENGTH!"

Until finally…

Predator Chase (Chapter 5)

A red predator fish hunts the crab across the reef — and the crab outlasts it.

Gameplay

Survive the patrolling fish for 45 seconds.

"SURVIVE THE PATROLLING FISH!"

And ever since then…

Home Sweet Home (Chapter 6)

The crab lives with its family in a protected coral sanctuary.

Gameplay

Run to your family.

"REUNITED! YOU ARE HOME."

SPACE X

Journey to Jupiter: four Levels follow the Hero's Mission, from the launch pad to Europa's hidden ocean and on to Jupiter.

Once upon a time…

The Mission

Far beyond Mars, Jupiter's icy moon Europa hides an ocean under its ice. Could it hold life?

Gameplay

"The Hero's mission: fly to Europa, Jupiter's icy moon, and find out if its hidden ocean could hold life."

Press SPACE to start.

One day…

Launch Pad (Level 1)

The Countdown starts. The Hero has 30 seconds to pack and board the Ship.

Gameplay

Walk left and right, collect the 4-item Checklist (helmet, ammo crate, lucky football, fuel can) and board the Ship before the Countdown ends. Then blast off!

Because of that…

Earth Orbit (Level 2)

The Ship reaches orbit, where old satellites and rocket parts are flying everywhere.

Gameplay

Dodge 10 pieces of space junk with 3 hearts. The Ship cannot shoot here.

Because of that…

Asteroid Belt (Level 3)

Between Mars and Jupiter, the Ship has to blast a path through the rocks.

Gameplay

Shoot 30 asteroids. The Ship fires footballs by itself every half second, SPACE adds extra shots, and the asteroids come faster as you score.

Until finally…

Europa (Level 4)

The Ship reaches Europa, breaks through the flying ice and scans the moon.

Gameplay

Break 12 ice chunks; each takes two hits, and the first hit cracks it. Then the Ship scans Europa for 3 seconds.

"Ocean found!"

And ever since then…

You Reached Jupiter!

The Mission is complete, and the Hero knows Europa has a giant salty ocean.

Gameplay

"Mission complete! The Hero scanned Europa's ocean and reached Jupiter."

See your score and high score, then press R to fly again.
