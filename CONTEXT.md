# Space X

An educational space game where a hero travels from Earth to Jupiter and learns about each place on the way.

## Language

**Hero**:
The astronaut pilot (Egypt suit, football) who wants to reach Jupiter. Seen on the intro, fact cards, win and game-over screens, never flown directly.
_Avoid_: Player character, astronaut sprite

**Ship**:
The spacecraft the player moves left and right and shoots from during a Level.
_Avoid_: Player, rocket

**Journey**:
The whole run from Earth to Jupiter, made of Levels played in order.
_Avoid_: Campaign, story mode

**Level**:
One stop on the Journey with its own background, its own challenge and its own Fact card. Order: Earth, Asteroid belt, Europa, then Jupiter as the ending.
_Avoid_: Stage, world, chapter

**Challenge**:
The one thing a Level asks the player to do. Asteroids are the Challenge only of the Asteroid belt Level.
_Avoid_: Mission, objective

**Fact card**:
A pause screen shown when a Level is cleared, teaching one thing about that place.
_Avoid_: Info screen, popup

**Intro**:
The first screen, where the Hero boards the Ship before launching into Level 1.
_Avoid_: Cutscene, splash
