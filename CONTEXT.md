# Space X

An educational space game where a hero travels from Earth to Jupiter and learns about each place on the way.

## Language

**Hero**:
The astronaut (Egypt suit, football) who wants to reach Jupiter. The player walks him on the Launch pad; after that he pilots the Ship and appears on the Title screen, Fact cards, win and game-over screens.
_Avoid_: Player character, astronaut sprite

**Ship**:
The spacecraft the Hero boards at the end of the Launch pad, and that the player moves and shoots from in every later Level.
_Avoid_: Player, rocket

**Mission**:
Why the Hero travels: to find out whether Europa's hidden ocean could hold life, then reach Jupiter. Told in short story lines on the Title screen, each Fact card and the win screen.
_Avoid_: Quest, objective, plot

**Journey**:
The whole run from the Launch pad to Jupiter, made of Levels played in order.
_Avoid_: Campaign, story mode

**Level**:
One stop on the Journey with its own background, its own Challenge and its own Fact card. Order: Launch pad, Earth orbit, Asteroid belt, Europa, then Jupiter as the ending.
_Avoid_: Stage, world, chapter

**Launch pad**:
Level 1, on the ground at the rocket station on Earth, where the Hero walks to the Ship.
_Avoid_: Space station, Earth level

**Earth orbit**:
Level 2, just above Earth, where the Ship dodges space junk.
_Avoid_: Earth level, low orbit

**Challenge**:
The one thing a Level asks the player to do. Asteroids are the Challenge only of the Asteroid belt Level.
_Avoid_: Mission, objective

**Checklist**:
The four items the Hero must pick up on the Launch pad before the Ship's door opens: helmet, ammo crate (footballs for the Ship to shoot), lucky football and fuel can.
_Avoid_: Inventory, pickups, collectibles

**Scan**:
The calm moment at the end of the Europa Level when the Ship scans the ice and finds the hidden ocean.
_Avoid_: Probe, analysis

**Countdown**:
The launch timer on the Launch pad. If it reaches zero before the Hero boards, the Level is lost.
_Avoid_: Timer, clock

**Fact card**:
A pause screen shown when a Level is cleared, teaching one thing about that place and telling what happens next in the Mission.
_Avoid_: Info screen, popup

**Title screen**:
The first screen of the game, separate from every Level: logo, the Hero, the Mission and "press Space to start".
_Avoid_: Intro, start menu, splash
