OUR GAMES

Two educational pixel-art games, built in Python with Pygame Zero: one about our ocean, one about our solar system.

Crab Quest

An ocean-themed educational adventure for ages 8–14. Play a crab escaping a kitchen counter, dodging seabirds and plastic trash, and coming home to a protected coral sanctuary.

Adventure Ages 8–14 6 chapters

Space X

An educational space shooter for ages 6–15. Steer and fire through asteroid waves across the solar system, and learn a fact about a planet every time you lose.

THE STUDIO PHILOSOPHY

We make curated, crafted adventures that feel like a quest and play like a lesson — pixel art drawn by hand, gameplay tested on real controllers, stories that stay with you.

01

HANDCRAFTED PIXEL ART

Every sprite, background, and splash frame is drawn by hand. No AI shortcuts, no generic asset packs — pure pixel craft.

02

THE HERO'S HEART

We build each game around the classic hero's journey. Our protagonists fail, learn, transform, and come home with something to share.

03

COMMUNITY-FIRST CRAFT

We build in the open. Iterative playtest builds and player feedback shape the chapters, pacing, and difficulty as we go.
